Step 1 — the twelve values

Property mode0 mode1
num_lanes 2 2
csi_pixel_bit_depth 10 10
active_w 3840 1920
active_h 2160 1080
max_framerate 30000000 60000000
pix_clk_hz 300000000 300000000
  • The values are those of the stock overlay in the r39.2.1 tree, lines 205 to 279.
  • max_framerate has a factor of one million, so 30000000 is 30 frames in a second.
  • Both modes declare the same pixel clock. Step 4 asks why that is correct.

Step 2 — the worksheet

# Mode Pixels in a second Bits in a second Bits in a second for each lane Part of 1500 Mbps
a IMX477 mode0, 3840×2160 at 30 248,832,000 2,488,320,000 1,244,160,000 82.9%
b IMX477 mode1, 1920×1080 at 60 124,416,000 1,244,160,000 622,080,000 41.5%
c virtcam mode0, 1920×1080 at 30 62,208,000 622,080,000 311,040,000 20.7%
  • All three fit. Row (a) has the least room: 17.1% of a lane is free.
  • The rates of the three rows are 4 : 2 : 1. Half the pixels at twice the rate give half the load, and half the rate gives half again.
  • A row that is 1.6 times too high used the 16 bits of the memory layout.

Step 3 — the baseline

  • The frame rate stays near 60, because mode1 is the 60-frame mode.
  • The counts of frame starts and frame ends are within a few of 3600. The capture starts and stops between two events, so they are not equal.
  • The error count is 0 on a healthy link, and dmesg has no new camera line.
  • The file is the reference for Lesson 20. A later fault is judged against these numbers.

Step 4 — the two answers

Why the declared clock is larger

  • The worksheet counts the pixels of the picture. The pixel clock of the sensor also runs during the blanking, between lines and between frames.
  • The mode node shows it: line_length is 11200 clocks for a line of 3840 pixels.
  • So the declared clock is always at least the pixel rate of the picture. The same clock serves both modes of this sensor, and the blanking takes the difference.

What a lowered clock does

  • The kernel computes the rate of a lane from the declared clock. The camera firmware programs the receiver for that rate.
  • With a lower number, the receiver blocks run for a slower link than the one that delivers the data.
  • The small mode can still work, because it has room. The 4K mode then has CRC errors or timeouts from time to time.
  • The fault looks like a signal problem, and it is one number in the device tree.
