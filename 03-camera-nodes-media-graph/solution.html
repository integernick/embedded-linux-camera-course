  • The drawing: imx477 9-001a → nvcsi channel → vi-output. Data flows left to right, as in the diagram of section 3 of the lesson.
  • The I2C sensor driver creates imx477 9-001a.
  • The NVCSI channel and the vi-output node belong to the NVCSI and tegra-camrtc-capture-vi platform drivers, which probed from the base device tree. The overlay of Lesson 02 only connected them to the sensor through endpoints.
  • Under a dual-camera overlay the CAM0 sensor is 10-001a, and the graph is the same (Lesson 02, section 7).
  • The entity type of the video node is Node subtype V4L, not a subdev. It is the one entity that is not a processing stage.
  • On kernel 6.8 the NVCSI entity name carries its channel number, -0, because the subdev name buffer has 52 bytes. The recorded name on a flashed r39.2.1 board is (verified-on-target: pending).

Mechanical verification

  • verify-build-jp7.sh runs inside jetson-build-jp7 with TREE at the pristine r39.2.1 tree. It re-runs the container transcripts of the sections. Those are the length of the NVCSI entity name, the devnode lines, and the kernel lines that the lesson prints.
  • verify-build-rpi.sh checks the Raspberry Pi parts of the sections against the pinned Raspberry Pi tree: the eight video nodes and the two link flags.
  • Neither script writes into the trees. Both finish in seconds.
