The sections of the lesson showed the three kinds of device node and the graph. This lab is the board work. You list the nodes of the camera of the devkit and read its real graph. Then you tie each entity to its driver.

Goal

  • A filled map of the media graph of the devkit, each entity tied to its driver and its device node.
  • The recorded name of the NVCSI entity on your board.

Hardware

  • Orin Nano devkit with JetPack 7.2.1 (Jetson Linux r39.2.1), reachable over SSH. BOARD_USER and BOARD_IP are exported as in Lab 01.
  • The IMX477 on CAM0, enabled since the lab of Lesson 02. ls /dev/video* shows /dev/video0 after boot.
  • No container. Every step runs on the board.

Steps

Step 1 — Install the tool (5 min)

  1. Install v4l-utils, the package that holds media-ctl and v4l2-ctl:
orin$ sudo apt install v4l-utils

apt prints its install log. Both tools are then on the path.

Step 2 — List the nodes and map the graph (15 min)

  1. List the device nodes:
orin$ ls -l /dev/video* /dev/media* /dev/v4l-subdev*

Expected: one /dev/video0, one /dev/media0 and two /dev/v4l-subdev* nodes, all with major 81. If /dev/video0 is missing, the overlay is not applied. Return to the lab of Lesson 02.

  1. Dump the graph:
orin$ media-ctl -p -d /dev/media0

Expected: three entities and two links marked [ENABLED], as in section 3 of the lesson.

  1. Fill in this worksheet from the dump. It is the first deliverable of the lab:
Entity name (from the dump) Kind /dev node Its source pad links to

The kinds are: sensor subdev, NVCSI channel subdev, video node. Answer two questions in writing. Which entity does the I2C sensor driver create? Which two entities belong to platform drivers that the overlay of Lesson 02 did not create?

  1. Draw the three entities with arrows in the direction of the data flow.

  2. Record the NVCSI entity name exactly as printed. Section 3 of the lesson expects 13e00000.host1x:nvcsi@15a00000-0 on kernel 6.8. Your record confirms or corrects that expectation.

On a Raspberry Pi 5

This lab has no Raspberry Pi form yet, and nothing of it is verified on a board (verified-on-target: pending).

  • Step 2. media-ctl -p prints more entities: the sensor, the CSI-2 receiver, the front end and eight video nodes. Only the link from the sensor is marked [ENABLED].

Expected output

  1. The worksheet. The three-row entity table with each row tied to its /dev node.
  2. The two written answers and the drawing of Step 2.
  3. The recorded name of the NVCSI entity.

Troubleshooting

Symptom Likely cause Fix
No /dev/video0 at all The overlay of Lesson 02 is not applied, or the wrong extlinux entry booted Repeat the verification of the lab of Lesson 02: extlinux.conf, reboot, dmesg | grep imx477
Permission denied on /dev/video0 The user is not in the video group Use sudo for the commands, or run sudo usermod -aG video $USER and log in again
media-ctl: command not found v4l-utils is not installed Repeat Step 1
